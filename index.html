4. Follow-up and investigations

Disposition planning
Safety rails and reminders
ECG and STEMI ECG within 10 minutes for suspected ACS. Manage STEMI on the dedicated pathway and repeat ECG when pain changes or with troponin draws.
Tool scope This helper supports the hs-troponin decision algorithm only; it does not replace ECG interpretation or clinical review.
Sampling rules Do not mix assays. The 3 h sample is 3 hours from the 0h troponin. If 0h troponin is <5 ng/L, use a 2 h sample when presentation is ≤60 min from onset; otherwise use a 1 h sample.
Unstable angina Ongoing or crescendo typical cardiac pain can still represent unstable angina; discuss with a senior clinician ± Cardiology regardless of troponin results.
